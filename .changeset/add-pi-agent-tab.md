Add pi-agent-tab: `agent` launches a pi subagent in a background Rex tab; it writes a report and wakes the parent over intercom. Modes (`scout`, `worker`, `review`, or your own markdown files in `~/.pi/agent/agent-modes/` and `.pi/agent-modes/`) preset the model, tools, skills and a role prompt.
